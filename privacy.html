Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and your choices.

Last updated: October 2026

LifeHub is a directory that helps people in Lagos find trusted artisans. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Artisans who list with us: your name, phone number, email (if you give one), trade, area, years of experience, short bio and photos of your work. Your name, trade, area, bio, photos and phone number are shown publicly on LifeHub so customers can contact you.
  • People who send us a message or report: the name, email and message you submit on our contact form.
  • People who leave a review: your rating, comment and the name you choose to show.
  • Contact activity: when someone taps "Contact" on an artisan, we record which artisan and which page. We do not record who the person is.
  • Usage data: we use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they use. It uses cookies and collects data such as your device type, browser and approximate location.

Why we use it

  • To review artisans and show them to customers.
  • To let customers contact artisans directly.
  • To reply to messages and look into reports.
  • To understand how LifeHub is used and to improve it.

Who can see it

Artisan listings are public once approved. Messages, reports and contact activity are visible only to the LifeHub team. We do not sell your information. We use trusted services to run LifeHub: Supabase (database and photo storage), Netlify (hosting) and Google Analytics. These providers process data for us and may store it on servers outside Nigeria.

Reviews

Reviews are checked by us before they appear. We may remove reviews that are false, abusive or unrelated to a job.

How long we keep it

We keep artisan listings while they are active. Messages and reports are kept for as long as we need them to handle the issue. You can ask us to remove your information at any time.

Your choices

  • Ask us to correct or remove your listing or any information about you.
  • Ask what information we hold about you.
  • Turn off cookies in your browser settings. LifeHub will still work.

To make a request, use our contact page. We handle personal information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Children

LifeHub is not meant for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
