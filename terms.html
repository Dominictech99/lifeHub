What LifeHub is

LifeHub is a directory that helps customers find artisans in Lagos. We are not the employer, agent or contractor of any artisan. When you hire someone you find here, the agreement is between you and that artisan only.

What "reviewed" and "Verified" mean

Before listing an artisan we look at the details and photos they send us. This is not a guarantee of their skill, qualifications, licence, background or behaviour. A "Founding Artisan" badge means the artisan was among the first 50 to join LifeHub. Please use your own judgement before hiring anyone.

Staying safe when you hire

  • Agree the price and the scope of the job before work starts.
  • Ask to see more of their past work if you want extra confidence.
  • Pay once you are satisfied, not the full amount upfront.
  • If something feels wrong, report it to us.

Payments

LifeHub does not handle payments between customers and artisans. We are not responsible for payments, prices, refunds or disputes.

For customers and reviewers
